Common questions

The widget shows nothing, or the same verse every day

Widgets are refreshed by iOS, not by the app. Remove the widget and add it again, then open Deepam once so it can rebuild its schedule. The verse changes at midnight, local time.

How do I set up the pause before the feed?

  1. Open Apple’s Shortcuts app → Automation → +.
  2. Choose App, pick the social app, select Is Opened and Run Immediately.
  3. Add the action Verse pause from Deepam.
  4. Tap the blue App field inside that action and pick the same app again. This step is required: it is how Deepam knows where to send you back.

Step-by-step instructions are also in the app: Places → Pause before the feed.

I tapped “Continue” and stayed in Deepam

The app field in the action is empty. Open the automation and pick the app inside the action, as in step 4 above.

The Control Center button is missing

It needs iOS 18 or newer. On iOS 17 the Lock Screen, Home Screen and StandBy widgets all work as usual.

How do I show or hide the Sanskrit text?

Look → App → Show the Sanskrit text. The source of the text and the translation is listed under Look → App → Text and translation.

How do I change the app icon?

Look → App → App icon. The main icon and Night are free; the others come with Deepam Pro. iOS confirms every change with its own message, which is normal.

How do I stop news and offers?

Places → In the moment → News and updates. It is off unless you turned it on. The verse of the day is a separate switch right above it.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Deepam. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your access continues until the end of the period you paid for. To restore a purchase on a new phone: the paywall → Restore purchases.

Is my data private?

Yes: no account, no ads, and your choices stay on your phone. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
